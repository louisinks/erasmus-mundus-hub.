Scholarship basics

The standard EMJM scholarship is €1,400 per month for the funded duration, generally up to 24 months. Scholarship holders are not charged mandatory participation costs.

Letter writing centre

Motivation letter

  1. Open with a concrete academic question or problem.
  2. Provide evidence from a relevant project, course or job.
  3. Connect specific programme modules and mobility choices to your goals.
  4. Show your contribution with verifiable examples.
  5. Close with a realistic career or research direction.

Suggested 500–650 words only if the programme gives no different instructions.

Recommendation letter

  1. Referee's relationship to applicant and duration.
  2. Academic or professional capabilities.
  3. Specific examples the referee witnessed.
  4. Readiness for the named programme.
  5. Independent endorsement and contact details.

The referee must authorise and independently assess the contents.

Referee request email framework

Subject: Request for academic recommendation – [Programme Name] Dear Dr [Surname], I am preparing an application for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's in [Programme Name] and would be grateful if you would consider providing an academic recommendation. Having studied [course/project] under your supervision, I believe you could comment on my work in [specific area]. The deadline is [date], and the submission process is [instructions]. I can share my CV, transcript and programme information. Please let me know whether you would be comfortable supporting my application. Kind regards, [Full Name]

Do not fabricate achievements or submit AI-generated material where the programme prohibits it.

Step-by-step application guide

  1. Search the official catalogue for 5–8 academically relevant programmes.
  2. Shortlist 3–5 after checking exact prerequisites, funding and deadlines.
  3. Request your transcript, degree certificate and English-medium instruction evidence if relevant.
  4. Choose referees who know your actual work and brief them early.
  5. Prepare a factual CV and tailored motivation letter for each programme.
  6. Apply via each consortium's official portal before its scholarship deadline.
  7. Check referee submissions and prepare for interviews where applicable.
  8. After an offer, follow consortium instructions on acceptance, insurance, housing and immigration.
