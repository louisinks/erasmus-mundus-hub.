Letter Review & Compliance Checker

Review a draft against programme-specific requirements and practical writing standards. All analysis runs in your browser; no letter is uploaded to a server.

These are applicant-entered requirements. The checker cannot independently verify that they match the official programme rules.

How to use the results

Check the official programme page first, copy the exact word limit and mandatory prompts, then run the review. A green result means only that a basic automated test passed. It does not establish academic quality, factual accuracy, compliance with all programme rules, or scholarship eligibility.

For deeper evaluation, ask a qualified reviewer to examine the letter against the programme's selection rubric and the applicant's CV. Do not submit fabricated claims or AI-generated writing where prohibited.
